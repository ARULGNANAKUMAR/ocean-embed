North Indian Ocean Intelligence Platform

Smart India Hackathon — AI Ocean Digital Twin

Mission

This platform reconstructs subsurface ocean temperature across 15 depth levels (0–1000m) for the North Indian Ocean using satellite-derived surface observations (SST, SSS, SLA, winds, currents) fed through a physics-guided CNN encoder–decoder architecture with spatial attention. It detects marine heatwaves, predicts their downstream impacts, and validates every prediction independently against ARGO float observations — which are never used in training.

Data Engineering

Phase 1–2: dataset scanning, QC, harmonization to a common 0.25° daily grid across 15 standard depths.

AI Core

Phase 3: CNN satellite embedding, physics-guided loss, marine heatwave intelligence, explainable AI, uncertainty quantification.

Digital Twin

Full-stack Flask + Three.js holographic Earth with live ARGO floats, depth diving, and voice-driven navigation.

Tech Stack

Backend: Flask · Flask-SocketIO · SQLite · PyTorch · Xarray · Pandas · NumPy
Frontend: HTML5 · CSS3 · JavaScript ES6 · Three.js · Chart.js · GSAP-style CSS animation
